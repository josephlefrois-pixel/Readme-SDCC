It bridges active field leadership on the City of San Diego municipal green waste contract into academic enrollment, assistive technology onboarding via the California Department of Rehabilitation (DOR), and paid student employment at San Diego City College (SDCC).

## 3. DOR Low-Vision Assistive Technology Specification
Medical and vocational documentation framework for California Department of Rehabilitation intake:

* **Clinical Metrics:** Right eye visual acuity 20/200; non-functional left eye; absent binocular fusion; severe asthenopia and decompensation at 30 minutes of continuous screen work.
* **Environmental Barriers:** High sensitivity to positive-polarity screen glare (white backgrounds wash out text); severe neck flexion strain when reading within 1–2 inches of display.
* **Mandated Assistive Hardware:**
  * 32-inch 4K UHD matte-finish display panel with high dynamic contrast.
  * Heavy-duty articulating gas-spring monitor arm allowing direct focal distance adjustment.
  * ANSI Z87.1 polarized UV-protective eyewear for high-glare environments.
* **Mandated Assistive Software:**
  * Freedom Scientific Fusion (ZoomText magnification 8x–16x paired with JAWS screen-reading audio offloading).
  * High-contrast system polarity (amber/yellow on black).
  * Speech-to-text input dictation software (Dragon Professional).
